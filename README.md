# rOtring Tikky Cross-Dating

The world's first cross-dating tool for rOtring Tikky mechanical pencils (1st generation, 1979-1998).

Cross-check the production era and authenticity of your Tikky by selecting its features: model, body color, barrel imprint, clip engraving, cap shape and more. When sources disagree on a date range, all opinions are shown side by side — the tool does not pick a side.

## Features

- **Trilingual UI**: English / 中文 / 日本語 (switch in the top-right corner)
- **Multi-criterion cross-checking**: model, color, imprint, clip, cap, tip ridges, internal plastic color
- **Cross-referenced dating**: three independent chronologies are compared (A: industry rules, B: rOtring Museum engineer statements, C: Japanese collector实测)
- **Traceable sources**: every claim is tagged with a source key and linked to its original page (Knockology threads, rOtring Museum, Stationeria, bungu-talk, 1996 official catalog)
- **No guessing**: combinations with no reliable record are marked "No reliable data"

## Usage

Open the live demo above, or download `index.html` and open it in any browser. No installation, no internet needed after download (all data is embedded in the single file).

## Data sources

- Knockology forum threads (main thread #2048, automatics timeline, short history, timeline chart etc. — relaying rOtring Museum curator Dux's statements)
- rOtring Museum (rotringmuseum.com item pages and catalogs)
- Stationeria blog (Taiwan) — Tikky Special articles 051/055/059/079
- bungu-talk (Japan) — Tikky lineage memo
- 1996 official rOtring catalog

Each claim in the tool carries a source key (e.g. [K1], [M4], [N3]) resolved in the "Sources" section at the bottom of the page.
